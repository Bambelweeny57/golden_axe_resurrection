# 🎮 JAMMA Conversion Notes  
### Running the Resurrected Board in a Standard Arcade Cabinet

Once the CPU and ROMs are replaced, the Golden Axe PCB can be wired to JAMMA for cabinet testing.

---

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This guide assumes familiarity with arcade wiring.  
Incorrect wiring can damage the board or cabinet.

---

## 🔌 Required Items

- JAMMA fingerboard  
- Soldering tools  
- 5V and 12V regulated supply  
- Speaker wiring  
- Video RGB lines

---

## 🧷 Wiring Overview

Golden Axe uses:

- **RGB video**  
- **Mono audio**  
- **Standard joystick/button inputs**  
- **5V logic power**  
- **12V audio amp power**

Map each signal from the PCB edge connector to the JAMMA fingerboard.

---

## 📺 Video

- R → JAMMA pin 12  
- G → JAMMA pin 13  
- B → JAMMA pin 14  
- Sync → JAMMA pin P

---

## 🔊 Audio

- Audio out → JAMMA pin 10  
- Ground → JAMMA pin 1

---

## 🎮 Controls

Map joystick and buttons according to standard JAMMA layout.

---

## ✔️ Final Testing

1. Insert board into cabinet  
2. Power on  
3. Verify video sync  
4. Test controls  
5. Play the restored game!
